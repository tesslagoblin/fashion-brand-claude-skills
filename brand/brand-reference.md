## 4. Brand pillars
- Every piece is an artifact. Clothing and objects belong to a story, and the storybook ships with them.
- Adventure in the real world. Made for the renaissance faire, and just as much for leading your friends to your favorite bar.
- A living universe. Recurring characters, places and crews that grow across collections.

## 5. Voice
- Sounds like: adventurous, theatrical, whimsical, story-first, a little mischievous
- Habits: speaks from inside the world ("an artifact of the legendary cat pirate himself"), then lands the real details (fabric, fit, pockets)
- Story words: artifact, lore, crew, captain, tavern, coffers, set sail, adventure awaits
